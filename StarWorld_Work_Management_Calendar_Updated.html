      Sections 1 & 9 — Review Calendar Document

      Hierarchy & Review Cycle

      Overall Review Hierarchy

      Sales Executive → BM → RM → GM → MD

      Supporting functions: Purchase | Inventory | Finance | HR | Marketing | MIS/Digital Marketing → BM/RM/GM → MD where strategic

      Review System Flow

      Daily Monitoring → Identify Gap → Immediate Action
      ↓
      Weekly Performance Review → Analyse Gap → Corrective Action
      ↓
      Monthly Business Review → Evaluate · Departments · Management Decisions
      ↓
      Quarterly Strategic Review → Strategy · Direction · Major Decisions · Next-Quarter Plan

      MD Rule (Important)

      The MD should not become part of daily operational reviews. Routine issues resolve through:

      Sales Executive → BM → RM → GM

      Only critical or strategic matters should reach the MD (via GM).

      Connect Reviews to Task Management

      Every gap or decision from a review should create a task: Owner · Deadline · Reviewer · Evidence · Status (Completed / Rework / Closed). This stops meetings from being discussion-only.
